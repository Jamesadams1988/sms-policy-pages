James Adams AI Assistant SMS Program: Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the James Adams AI Assistant SMS Program: text messages sent by James Adams' AI assistant on his behalf from +1 636-668-0196. See the program page for how to opt in.

Information we collect

We collect only the mobile phone number, name, opt-in record (date and method of consent), and message content that you give us when you agree to receive texts or when you text +1 636-668-0196.

How we use it

We use this information only to send and manage the scheduling, reminder, and personal logistics messages you agreed to receive, to respond to your replies, and to honor HELP and STOP requests.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties. We do not sell or rent your information.

Our messaging and cloud service providers (such as our SMS carrier provider, Telnyx) process message data solely to deliver and secure the messages, and not for their own marketing.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. We will send one final confirmation, stop all messages, and remove your number from the approved-contacts list. Reply HELP for help, or email adams.grokbot@agentmail.to. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Retention and security

We keep your number only while you are opted in, plus a minimal record of your opt-in and opt-out as required by carrier rules. We use reasonable safeguards to protect it.
